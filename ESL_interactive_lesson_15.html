Lesson 15: Global Party Time!

Public Holidays & Celebrations

Learn to describe big celebrations and share your amazing holiday stories.

Target: Kids (A2-B1) Focus: Past Simple/Continuous & Sequence Words

I. Mission Goals (Storyteller Skills!)

By the end of this lesson, you can:

  • 🎉 Use new words for holiday **customs** and **traditions** (e.g., *parade*, *feast*, *lantern*).
  • 🕰️ Accurately use the **Past Simple** and **Past Continuous** to narrate events.
  • ➡️ Connect sentences using **sequence words** (e.g., *First*, *While*, *Finally*).
  • 🌎 Describe and **compare** different cultural celebrations.

II. Global Celebration Challenge (Activities)

A. Warm-up: What Do We See? (10-15 minutes)

Let's look at images of different global holidays and learn the vocabulary!

Celebration Vocabulary:

Tradition: Something done every year.
Feast: A very large, special meal.
Parade: A public procession with music.
Fireworks: Explosions of light in the sky.
Lantern: A paper light for decorating.
Costume: Special clothes for a party.

B. Activity 1: What Happened? (Past Simple vs. Continuous) (20-25 minutes)

We use the Past Tenses to tell a story about a finished holiday.

Past Simple (Finished Action):

Structure: **[Subject] + Verb-ed (or Irregular)**

Example: We opened the gifts.

Past Continuous (Ongoing Action / Background):

Structure: **[Subject] + was/were + Verb-ing**

Example: I was watching the movie when my dad called.

Activity: "Holiday Memory Match."

Match an ongoing action (Past Continuous) with a quick, finished action (Past Simple) that interrupted it. (e.g., **While** I was eating cake, the music stopped.)

C. Activity 2: Telling a Clear Story (Sequence Words) (15-20 minutes)

These words help us put the events of our story in the correct order.

Key Sequence Words:
  • **Beginning:** First, To begin with, After that.
  • **Middle:** Then, Next, While (for Past Continuous).
  • **End:** Finally, In the end.

Activity: "Holiday Step-by-Step."

Students write 5 holiday activities on separate cards. They mix them up, and then use the sequence words to put them back in the correct order as they tell the story.

D. Wrap-up: Compare Your Party! (10-15 minutes)

In pairs, students describe their favorite holiday using **Past Simple**, **Past Continuous**, and **Sequence Words**.

Example phrases to use:

  • "**First**, we **traveled** to the mountain." (**Past Simple**)
  • "**While** my grandmother **was cooking**, my brother and I **were playing** games." (**Past Continuous**)
  • "**Finally**, we **ate** a huge feast." (**Past Simple**)

III. Homework: "My Holiday Story"

Write a short paragraph about the last time you celebrated a big holiday. Make sure to use **Past Simple**, **Past Continuous**, and at least three **Sequence Words**.

Example:

First, we went to the town square. While the band was playing, I bought a balloon. Next, we found a good spot. Everyone was cheering loudly when the mayor spoke. Finally, the fireworks started!

"You're a great storyteller! Keep practicing those tenses."
