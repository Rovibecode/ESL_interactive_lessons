Lesson 12: Super Tech!

Technology & Communication

Talk about your devices, games, and being safe online.

Target: Kids (A2-B1) Focus: Phrasal Verbs & Imperatives

I. Mission Goals (Tech Skills!)

By the end of this lesson, you can:

  • 💻 Use new words for **devices and online activities** (e.g., *stream*, *tablet*).
  • 🔌 Use **technology phrasal verbs** (like *log in*, *switch off*).
  • ⚙️ Give and follow **simple instructions** using the **Imperative form**.
  • 🗣️ Talk about **communication methods** like texting and video calls.

II. Game Time! (Lesson Activities)

A. Warm-up: Name the Gadget! (10-15 minutes)

Let's learn the names for all the cool stuff we use!

Key Tech Vocabulary:

Smartphone: 智能手機
Tablet: 平板電腦
Stream: 串流/看影片
App: 應用程式
Chat: 聊天
Password: 密碼

B. Activity 1: Phrasal Verb Power! (20-25 minutes)

These are verb + small word combinations that we use all the time when talking about tech!

Tech Phrasal Verbs:

  • Log in: To start using a website or game. *Please **log in** before you play.*
  • Switch off/Turn off: To stop the power. *You need to **switch off** the tablet.*
  • Scroll up/down: To move the screen. *Can you **scroll down** to see the picture?*
  • Back up: To save your files safely. *I always **back up** my photos.*

Activity: "Mime the Verb."

Students take turns miming a phrasal verb (e.g., logging in, scrolling down) while their classmates guess the correct verb and say a sentence using it.

C. Activity 2: The Command Center (Imperatives) (20-25 minutes)

We use the **Imperative** form (just the base verb) to give simple instructions, advice, or warnings!

Rule Check! (Just the Verb!)

Positive: **Click** the button! **Open** the chat!

Negative: **Don't share** your password! **Do not touch** the screen!

Activity: "Tech Support Role-Play."

Students work in pairs. One is the "Tech User" with a simple problem (e.g., "The sound is off!"). The other is the "Tech Expert" who must give instructions using imperatives and phrasal verbs: "**Switch on** the speaker! **Don't worry**, I will fix it."

D. Wrap-up: Online Safety Rules! (5-10 minutes)

Let's create rules for being safe when we are online, all using imperatives.

Examples:

  • **Always ask** an adult.
  • **Never share** personal photos.
  • **Don't open** messages from people you don't know.

III. Homework: "My Tech Guide"

Write a short guide (3-5 steps) on how to use your favorite game or app. Use imperatives and phrasal verbs!

Instructions:

How to Win at My Favorite Game:

  1. First, **log in** to the game server.
  2. Next, **tap** the start button.
  3. **Don't forget** to **back up** your score!
  4. If you lose, **switch off** your tablet and try again later.

"Bring your guide next time so we can all follow your instructions!"
