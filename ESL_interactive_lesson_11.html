LEVEL UP!

Daily Life & Routines (Advanced Fun!)

Talk about habits, temporary actions, and super-fast adverbs!

Target: Kids (A2-B2) Focus: Idioms & Advanced Grammar

I. Mission Goals (What We'll Learn!)

By the end of this awesome lesson, you can:

  • ✨ Use cool idioms to talk about school and play (like *Hit the books*!).
  • 🚦 Know the difference between **I eat** (always) and **I am eating** (just now).
  • ⚡ Describe *how* you do things with exciting **Adverbs of Manner** (like *loudly* or *silently*).
  • 🏆 Talk about big routine changes using the **Present Perfect** (*I have played soccer*).

II. Game Time! (Lesson Activities)

A. Warm-up: Secret Code Words (Idioms) (10-15 minutes)

These secret words make your English sound super cool and fast!

Kid-Power Idioms:

Hit the sack: Go to bed. (去睡覺)
Catch up on: Do the things you missed (like sleep or homework). (補回來)
Hit the books: Study hard. (用功讀書)
In a flash: Do something super quickly. (一眨眼/很快)

B. Activity 1: The 'Always' vs. 'Just Now' Game (25-30 minutes)

We use **Simple** for things we *always* do, and **Continuous** for things we are *only doing right now* (or just for a little while).

Rule Check!

Always Habit: **I play** outside every day. (Simple)

Temporary Action: **I am playing** inside today because it **is raining**. (Continuous)

Super Adverbs (How?):

  • **Loudly:** *He shouts loudly when he wins.*
  • **Carefully:** *Please walk carefully on the wet floor.*
  • **Happily:** *She is singing happily right now.*
  • **Silently:** *I am reading silently in the library.*

Game: "Change the Plan!"

Students get a "Normal Day" card ("I usually **walk** home"). The teacher gives a "Problem Card" ("But today, there is a silly parade!"). Students must change the sentence to the Continuous and use an Adverb: "I **am skipping** home carefully today."

C. Activity 2: My Life Experiences (Present Perfect) (25-30 minutes)

We use this to talk about things that happened *sometime* in your life that are important *now*.

Rule Check! (Have/Has + Past Participle)

Experience Question: **Have you ever** eaten a sour pickle? (Yes/No, sometime in your life.)

Life Change: **I have never tried** ice skating before today. (Up to this moment.)

Activity: "The Experience Tag."

Students write down two things they **have never done** in their morning routine, and two things they **have always done**. They then ask a partner, "Have you ever...?" The partner must answer using the Present Perfect.

D. Wrap-up: Super Sentence Challenge! (5-10 minutes)

Let's make one huge sentence that uses everything we learned!

Example Super Sentence:

"Even though I **have always tried** to **hit the books** **diligently**, this week I **am spending** extra time playing **loudly** outside because I **am catching up on** my running practice."

III. Homework: "My Week Report"

Write a special report about your week! This is where you show off your new super words.

Instructions:

  1. Write **one paragraph** about your *normal* routine, then *one temporary change* you are doing this week.
  2. Use **three** of the new **idioms** (like *hit the books*).
  3. Use at least **three** **Adverbs of Manner** (like *quickly*, *happily*).
  4. Write one sentence about something you **have never done** before related to your routine.

"Ready to make your English strong? Good luck, Super Students!"
