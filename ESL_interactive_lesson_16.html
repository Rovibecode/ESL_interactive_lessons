Lesson 16: Anime Art Adventure!

Arts & Entertainment

Dive into movies, music, and art! Learn to share your feelings and opinions.

Target: Kids (A2-B1) Focus: -ed/-ing Adjectives & Cause/Effect

I. Mission Goals (Express Yourself!)

By the end of this lesson, you can:

  • 🎬 Identify different **genres** of entertainment (e.g., *action*, *fantasy*, *pop*).
  • 🎨 Describe types of **art** (e.g., *painting*, *sculpture*) and **emotional reactions**.
  • 🤩 Correctly use **-ed** (how you feel) and **-ing** (what causes the feeling) **adjectives**.
  • 🤔 Explain **cause and effect** when talking about art/entertainment.

II. Creative Quest (Activities)

A. Warm-up: What's Your Favorite? (10-15 minutes)

Let's explore different kinds of movies, music, and art!

Genres & Art Vocabulary:

Fantasy: Magic, dragons.
Sci-Fi: Space, robots.
Pop Music: Popular, catchy.
Painting: Art with colors.
Sculpture: 3D art.
Exciting: Causes excitement.

B. Activity 1: How Do You Feel? (20-25 minutes)

Use **-ed** adjectives for how *you* feel, and **-ing** adjectives for what *causes* the feeling!

Adjective Adventure:

I am excited because the movie is exciting.

She is bored because the book is boring.

He is interested because the painting is interesting.

Activity: "Feeling Face-Off."

Show cards with entertainment items (e.g., "a scary movie," "a slow song"). Students use **-ed** and **-ing** adjectives to describe their reaction and the item.

Example: "The movie is **scary**, so I am **scared**."

C. Activity 2: Why Did That Happen? (Cause and Effect) (15-20 minutes)

Use words like **because**, **so**, **as a result** to explain why something happens.

Cause & Effect Words:
  • **Cause:** The painting was beautiful because it used many colors.
  • **Effect:** The movie was exciting, so everyone cheered.
  • **Effect:** The music was very loud. As a result, my ears hurt!

Activity: "Chain Reaction Stories."

Students get a "cause" (e.g., "The song was fast"). They must create an "effect" sentence, then the next student uses that effect as a new cause for another effect, building a chain story.

D. Wrap-up: My Masterpiece! (10-15 minutes)

Students draw their favorite piece of art (or imagine one) and describe it using **-ed/-ing adjectives** and **cause/effect** sentences.

Example:

  • "This painting is so interesting! It makes me feel amazed."
  • "It's a very exciting picture because the colors are so bright."

III. Homework: "My Entertainment Review"

Choose your favorite movie, song, or book. Write a short review describing it and how it makes you feel!

Instructions:

My Review: "Space Heroes!"

  1. What is it? (Genre: Action/Sci-Fi movie)
  2. How does it make you feel? (e.g., "I am thrilled!")
  3. Why? (e.g., "The story is very thrilling, so I love it!")
  4. Use at least two **-ed/-ing** adjectives and one **cause/effect** word.

"You're an amazing critic! Keep sharing your thoughts!"
