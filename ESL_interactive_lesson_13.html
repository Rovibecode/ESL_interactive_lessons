Lesson 13: School Quest!

Education & Learning Paths

Talk about school subjects, good habits, and what you'll be learning next!

Target: Kids (A2-B1) Focus: Modals of Necessity & Future Continuous

I. Mission Goals (Academic Skills!)

By the end of this lesson, you can:

  • 📚 Name different **school subjects** and **study habits** (e.g., *revise*, *take notes*).
  • 🛑 Give **strong rules** using the **Modals of Necessity** (*must*, *have to*).
  • 🎓 Give **good advice** using the Modal *should*.
  • ⏳ Talk about what you **will be doing** at a specific time in the future (Future Continuous).

II. Game Time! (Lesson Activities)

A. Warm-up: My Awesome School Day! (10-15 minutes)

Let's talk about the subjects and habits that help us learn!

Key Study Vocabulary:

P.E. (Physical Education): 體育課
Art Class: 美術課
Science Lab: 科學實驗室
Take Notes: 做筆記
Review/Revise: 複習
Pay Attention: 專心

B. Activity 1: The School Rulebook (20-25 minutes)

We use **must/have to** for BIG rules, and **should** for helpful advice!

Rule Check! (Modals)

BIG Rule: You must listen to the teacher. / We have to wear a uniform.

Good Advice: You should go to bed early. / You shouldn't play video games all night.

Activity: "Must, Have To, Should."

Students get a card with a situation (e.g., "You have a test tomorrow"). They must create three sentences: one using **must/have to** (a strong rule), one using **should** (advice), and one using the negative **mustn't/shouldn't**.

Example: "I **must** study hard. I **should** ask my teacher for help. I **mustn't** talk to my friend during the test."

C. Activity 2: Next Year's Schedule! (20-25 minutes)

We use the **Future Continuous** to talk about an action that **will be happening** at a specific time later.

Rule Check! (Will be + Verb-ing)

Example: At 9 AM next Monday, I **will be taking** my Math class.

Example: This time next year, I **will be learning** about history.

Activity: "The Time Traveler."

Students pair up. One student names a future time (e.g., "1 PM next Saturday"). The other student must answer with the Future Continuous, describing a school-related activity they **will be doing** (or not doing) at that exact moment. They must also include a Modal: "**You should** be resting, but I **will be practicing** my Art homework."

D. Wrap-up: Best Study Advice! (5-10 minutes)

The class creates a giant poster of the best advice for a new student.

Examples:

  • "You **must** always **pay attention** in Science!"
  • "You **should** **review** your notes before bed."
  • "You **mustn't** forget to **hand in** your assignments."

III. Homework: "My Future Learning Plan"

Write a short plan about your learning goals for the next month!

Instructions:

My Plan:

  1. Write one thing you **must** do to pass your hardest class.
  2. Write one thing you **should** do to get better at your favorite subject.
  3. Write two sentences using the **Future Continuous** (e.g., "Next Tuesday at 4 PM, I **will be doing** my Math homework.").
  4. Include at least two new **Study Habit** words (e.g., *review*, *take notes*).

"Don't forget to **take notes** on this lesson so you can **review** it later!"
