ESL Lesson Plan: My City & Getting Around

Navigating Places and Giving Directions

Target: Kids & Adults Level: A1-B1 (CEFR) Time: 60-75 Mins Focus: City Places, Public Transport, Prepositions, Directions

I. Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • 🏙️ Identify and name common places in a city.
  • 🚌 Use vocabulary for different types of public transportation.
  • 📍 Correctly use prepositions of place to describe locations.
  • ➡️ Ask for and give simple directions.

II. Materials

  • Whiteboard or projector
  • Markers/pens
  • Pictures or flashcards of city places and transportation
  • Simple city map handout

III. Lesson Procedure

A. Warm-up: "Where Am I?" (10-15 minutes)

Students will identify different city places from pictures to introduce key vocabulary.

Introduction:

"Hello everyone! Today we are going to explore our city and learn how to get around!"

大家好！今天我們將探索我們的城市並學習如何四處走動！

(Dàjiā hǎo! Jīntiān wǒmen jiāng tànsuǒ wǒmen de chéngshì bìng xuéxí rúhé sìchù zǒudòng!)

Key Vocabulary:

Park: 公園 (gōngyuán)
School: 學校 (xuéxiào)
Hospital: 醫院 (yīyuàn)
Library: 圖書館 (túshūguǎn)
Restaurant: 餐廳 (cāntīng)
Supermarket: 超級市場 (chāojí shìchǎng)

B. Main Activities (40-45 minutes)

Interactive activities to practice city navigation, transportation, and giving directions.

Activity 1: "Public Transport & Prepositions"

Students will learn names of public transport and practice prepositions to describe location.

Vocabulary: Transportation & Prepositions:

Transportation:
  • Bus: 公車 (gōngchē)
  • Train: 火車 (huǒchē)
  • Subway: 捷運 (jiéyùn)
  • Taxi: 計程車 (jìchéngchē)
Prepositions of Place:
  • Next to: 在...旁邊 (zài...pángbiān)
  • Between: 在...之間 (zài...zhījiān)
  • Across from: 在...對面 (zài...duìmiàn)

Activity: "Describe the Map."

Give students a simple map with a few places and public transport routes. Ask them to describe where places are using prepositions. For example, "The library is **next to** the park."

Activity 2: "Giving & Asking for Directions"

Students will practice asking for and giving directions to get to different places on a map.

Phrases for Directions:

  • How do I get to the...? (我怎麼去...？)
  • Go straight. 直走 (zhí zǒu)
  • Turn left/right. 左轉/右轉 (zuǒ zhuǎn/yòu zhuǎn)
  • It's on your left/right. 它在你的左邊/右邊 (tā zài nǐ de zuǒbiān/yòubiān)
  • You can't miss it. 你不會錯過的 (nǐ bú huì cuòguò de)

Activity: Role-play "Tourist & Local."

In pairs, one student acts as a tourist asking for directions to a place on the map, and the other student acts as a local giving directions using the learned phrases.

C. Wrap-up: "My City Sightseeing" (5-10 minutes)

Students will describe a favorite place in their city and give simple directions on how to get there.

Examples:

"My favorite place is the **City Museum**. You can take the **bus number 5**." (我最喜歡的地方是城市博物館。你可以搭5號公車。) (Wǒ zuì xǐhuān de dìfāng shì chéngshì bówùguǎn. Nǐ kěyǐ dā 5 hào gōngchē.)

IV. Homework Assignment: "Map Your Trip"

Students will create a simple map and write out directions for a short trip in their city.

Instructions:

"Draw a simple map of your neighborhood or a part of your city. Mark a starting point and a destination. Write out the directions to get from the start to the end."

Example Trip:

"How to get from my house to the library."

"From my house, turn right onto Main Street. Go straight for two blocks. The library is on your left, next to the park."

Map Your Trip: 繪製你的旅程地圖 (huìzhì nǐ de lǚchéng dìtú)

"Please write your entries on a separate piece of paper or in your notebook."

請將你的作業寫在一張獨立的紙上或筆記本裡。

(Qǐng jiāng nǐ de zuòyè xiě zài yī zhāng dúlì de zhǐ shàng huò bǐjìběn lǐ.)
