ESL Lesson Plan: Shopping & Money

Navigating Stores and Making Purchases

Target: Kids & Adults Level: A1-B1 (CEFR) Time: 60-75 Mins Focus: Shopping Vocabulary, Quantities, Prices, Polite Requests

I. Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • 🛒 Identify and name common items found in different types of stores (e.g., supermarket, clothing store).
  • 💰 Use vocabulary related to prices and money.
  • 📊 Apply quantifiers (e.g., some, any, much, many) correctly for countable and uncountable nouns.
  • 🗣️ Ask and answer questions about items and prices.
  • 🙏 Make polite requests and express needs when shopping.
  • 🛍️ Engage in simple shopping role-plays.

II. Materials

  • Whiteboard or projector
  • Markers/pens
  • Pictures/flashcards of various shopping items (groceries, clothes, electronics)
  • Play money (optional, for role-play)
  • Worksheet for Activity 2 and Homework (to be provided by teacher)

III. Lesson Procedure

A. Warm-up: "What Did You Buy Recently?" (10-15 minutes)

Students will share a recent purchase to introduce basic shopping vocabulary.

Introduction:

"Hello shoppers! Today, we're going to talk about 'Shopping and Money'!"

各位購物者們好！今天我們要聊聊「購物與金錢」！

(Gèwèi gòuwù zhěmen hǎo! Jīntiān wǒmen yào liáoliáo “gòuwù yǔ jīnqián”!)

Key Vocabulary:

Shop/Store: 商店 (shāngdiàn)
Buy: 買 (mǎi)
Sell: 賣 (mài)
Price: 價格 (jiàgé)
Money: 錢 (qián)
Discount: 折扣 (zhékòu)
Cash: 現金 (xiànjīn)
Credit Card: 信用卡 (xìnyòngkǎ)

B. Main Activities (40-45 minutes)

Interactive activities to practice shopping vocabulary, quantities, and polite requests.

Activity 1: "At the Supermarket: Quantities & Groceries"

Students will practice identifying groceries and using quantifiers for countable and uncountable nouns.

Vocabulary: Groceries & Quantifiers:

Groceries:
  • Apple: 蘋果 (píngguǒ)
  • Milk: 牛奶 (niúnǎi)
  • Bread: 麵包 (miànbāo)
  • Eggs: 雞蛋 (jīdàn)
  • Water: 水 (shuǐ)
Quantifiers:
  • Some: 一些 (yīxiē)
  • Any: 任何 (rènhé)
  • A lot of: 很多 (hěnduō)
  • Much (uncountable): 很多 (hěnduō)
  • Many (countable): 許多 (xǔduō)

Examples:

"I need some milk and a lot of apples." (我需要一些牛奶和很多蘋果。) (Wǒ xūyào yīxiē niúnǎi hé hěnduō píngguǒ.)

"Do you have any eggs? How much water do you want?" (你們有雞蛋嗎？你想要多少水？) (Nǐmen yǒu jīdàn ma? Nǐ xiǎng yào duōshǎo shuǐ?)

Activity: "Shopping List Game."

Provide students with pictures of supermarket items. Call out items and quantities, and students say if they have "some," "a lot of," "much," or "many."

Activity 2: "Asking Prices & Polite Requests"

Students will practice asking about prices and making polite requests in shopping scenarios.

Phrases for Prices & Requests:

  • How much is this? (這個多少錢？)
  • What's the price of...? (...的價格是多少？)
  • Can I try this on? (我能試穿這個嗎？)
  • Could I have...? (我能拿/要...嗎？)
  • I'd like to buy... (我想買...)
  • Do you have this in a different size/color? (這個有不同尺寸/顏色嗎？)

Activity: Role-play "Shopping Trip."

Set up two scenarios: a clothing store and an electronics store. Students role-play as customers and shopkeepers, practicing asking prices, trying on clothes, and making polite requests for items.

C. Wrap-up: "My Dream Purchase" (5-10 minutes)

Students will describe one item they would like to buy, using new vocabulary and phrases.

Examples:

"I'd like to buy a new laptop. It's expensive, but I need it for work." (我想買一台新筆記型電腦。它很貴，但我工作需要它。) (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yì tái xīn bǐjìxíng diànnǎo. Tā hěn guì, dàn wǒ gōngzuò xūyào tā.)

"I want to buy some new shoes. They are very comfortable, and I saw them on discount." (我想買一些新鞋。它們很舒服，而且我看到它們有折扣。) (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yīxiē xīn xié. Tāmen hěn shūfu, érqiě wǒ kàndào tāmen yǒu zhékòu.)

IV. Homework Assignment: "My Shopping Trip Journal"

Students will describe a shopping experience, applying new vocabulary and phrases.

Instructions:

"Write 5-7 sentences for the following entries:"

1. My Recent Shopping Trip:

Describe a recent time you went shopping. Where did you go? What did you buy? How much did it cost? Use phrases for quantities and prices.

Recent Shopping Trip: 最近的購物之旅 (zuìjìn de gòuwù zhī lǚ)

2. Comparing Two Items:

Choose two similar items (e.g., two different brands of milk, two types of shirts). Compare their prices, quality, or features. Which one would you prefer to buy and why?

Comparing Items: 比較商品 (bǐjiào shāngpǐn)

"Please write your entries on a separate piece of paper or in your notebook."

請將你的作業寫在一張獨立的紙上或筆記本裡。

(Qǐng jiāng nǐ de zuòyè xiě zài yī zhāng dúlì de zhǐ shàng huò bǐjìběn lǐ.)
