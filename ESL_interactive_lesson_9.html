ESL Lesson Plan: Health & Wellness

Feeling Good and Staying Healthy

Target: Kids & Adults Level: A1-B1 (CEFR) Time: 60-75 Mins Focus: Body Parts, Ailments, Advice, Habits

I. Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • 💪 Identify and name major body parts.
  • 🤧 Describe common ailments and feelings.
  • 🍎 Discuss and recommend healthy habits.
  • 💬 Use modal verbs like "should" and "shouldn't" to give and receive advice.

II. Materials

  • Whiteboard or projector
  • Markers/pens
  • Worksheet with pictures of body parts and a health advice matching game
  • Flashcards of common ailments and healthy habits

III. Lesson Procedure

A. Warm-up: "Touch Your..." (10-15 minutes)

Students will follow simple commands to identify and name different body parts.

Introduction:

"Hello everyone! Today, let's learn how to talk about our bodies and how to stay healthy!"

大家好！今天我們來學習如何談論我們的身體以及如何保持健康！

(Dàjiā hǎo! Jīntiān wǒmen lái xuéxí rúhé tánlùn wǒmen de shēntǐ yǐjí rúhé bǎochí jiànkāng!)

Key Vocabulary:

Head: 頭 (tóu)
Arm: 手臂 (shǒubì)
Leg: 腿 (tuǐ)
Hand: 手 (shǒu)
Foot: 腳 (jiǎo)
Stomach: 肚子 (dùzi)

B. Main Activities (40-45 minutes)

Interactive activities to practice talking about health issues and giving advice.

Activity 1: "Doctor, Doctor!"

Students will learn vocabulary for common ailments and use it to describe how they feel.

Vocabulary: Common Ailments & Feelings:

Ailments:
  • Headache: 頭痛 (tóutòng)
  • Fever: 發燒 (fāshāo)
  • Stomachache: 肚子痛 (dùzi tòng)
  • Sore throat: 喉嚨痛 (hóulóng tòng)
Feelings:
  • Tired: 累 (lèi)
  • Sick: 生病 (shēngbìng)
  • Dizzy: 頭暈 (tóuyūn)
  • Pain: 疼痛 (téngtòng)

Activity: "Describe the Symptom."

Show flashcards of ailments. Students must describe the problem using the vocabulary. Example: "I have a headache." "I feel dizzy."

Activity 2: "Giving Advice"

Students will practice giving health advice using "should" and "shouldn't".

Phrases for Giving Advice:

  • You should... 你應該... (nǐ yīnggāi...)
  • You shouldn't... 你不應該... (nǐ bù yīnggāi...)
  • Maybe you could... 也許你可以... (yěxǔ nǐ kěyǐ...)
  • It's a good idea to... ...是個好主意 (...shì gè hǎo zhǔyì)

Activity: "What Should I Do?"

Students present a health problem (e.g., "I have a terrible cold."). Other students take turns giving advice using "should" and "shouldn't".

C. Wrap-up: "My Healthy Habits" (5-10 minutes)

Students will share a healthy habit they have or want to adopt.

Examples:

"I think everyone should drink more water." (我認為每個人都應該多喝水。) (Wǒ rènwéi měi gèrén dōu yīnggāi duō hē shuǐ.)

"To stay healthy, you should eat lots of vegetables." (為了保持健康，你應該多吃蔬菜。) (Wèile bǎochí jiànkāng, nǐ yīnggāi duō chī shūcài.)

IV. Homework Assignment: "Health Journal"

Students will describe a past health issue and the advice they received, or write about their own healthy habits.

Instructions:

"Choose one of the two topics below and write 5-7 sentences."

1. When I Was Sick:

Write about a time you were sick. What was the problem? What did someone say you should do?

My Health Story: 我的健康故事 (wǒ de jiànkāng gùshì)

2. My Healthy Routine:

Describe a few things you do to stay healthy. Use "should" and "shouldn't" to give advice to others.

Healthy Habits: 健康習慣 (jiànkāng xíguàn)

"Please write your entries on a separate piece of paper or in your notebook."

請將你的作業寫在一張獨立的紙上或筆記本裡。

(Qǐng jiāng nǐ de zuòyè xiě zài yī zhāng dúlì de zhǐ shàng huò bǐjìběn lǐ.)
